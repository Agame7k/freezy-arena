Field simulator

Everything here comes from the running Cheesy Arena code: the hub lights are the LED controller's own pixels, the signs show what the sign code would send, the stack light and hub motors/lights are the PLC outputs, and the screens are the real displays: one of every kind, configured the way it would be where it sits around the field (alliance station and drivers' field monitors on the walls, the FTA monitor and announcer display on the scoring table, the stream monitor and an unassigned spare on the A/V cart, the audience, wall, and bracket screens behind the table, and the queueing, rankings, web page, and logo displays on the audience side). They show up in Setup > Displays as 'Field Sim ...', and reconfiguring one there (for example the spare) changes it here too. The stream and web page displays load outside content, so they only load when you click them.

With no field PLC configured, a simulated PLC stands in so that the arena's PLC logic runs; its stop buttons and hub fuel sensors are the buttons here. Simulated robots connect over the same network protocol as a real Driver Station, as whichever teams are in the loaded match.

Getting started

  1. Press Start test match to run a match with no robots (every station is bypassed).
  2. For robots: press Use event teams, then All robots on, then Start test match. Robots that are linked get enabled by the arena; watch their signal lights blink.
  3. Score fuel, press stop buttons, or skip ahead (or click ahead on the timeline) to see what changes.
